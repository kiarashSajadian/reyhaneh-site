I’m Reyhaneh aka Reyna. For 7+ years I’ve built international organic growth through technical SEO, content systems and AI-powered automation — across marketplaces, legal tech and agency work in Europe, Australia and the Middle East.

little more about me :)

Engineer by training,
painter at heart,
growth marketer by choice.

I studied electrical telecommunications engineering, then earned a master’s in painting. That mix still shapes how I work: systems thinking and data on one side, storytelling and craft on the other.

I started in SEO in Tehran in 2018. Growing a website from 1K to 150K monthly visits gave me confidence in my craft, and soon after I was leading a four-person SEO team at an international legal platform. In Berlin I built programmatic SEO for Vinted across seven markets. Today I own SEO and GEO strategy at Helpling, focusing on how people find services in both classic search and AI-generated answers.

Outside my day job I judge the EU and Global Search Awards and teach SEO and GEO to postgraduate students. And I still paint! a small home studio keeps my creative side busy.
